## Existing local-news publishing behavior

The production importer already has these city feeds:

| City | Publisher RSS |
|---|---|
| Los Angeles | https://ktla.com/news/local-news/feed/ |
| San Diego | https://timesofsandiego.com/feed/ |
| San Jose | https://sanjosespotlight.com/feed/ |
| San Francisco | https://sfstandard.com/feed/ |
| Fresno | https://fresnoland.org/feed/ |

City matching rules filter unrelated feed entries. Feed selection favors sections with older coverage. Named city authors are assigned through `sectionWriter()`.

Production cron invokes `scripts/aggregate.js` every 30 minutes under `flock`, avoiding overlapping scheduled runs. Publication pacing currently restricts normal runs to 06:00–21:00 Pacific, with configured daily/per-run limits. Preserve these limits unless intentionally changing them; “cron every 30 minutes” does not mean publication throughout the night.

City articles use `kind: 'section-brief'`. They require successful AI rewriting, sanitized HTML and a second source-based factual review before insertion. Failures are skipped and can be retried while the item remains eligible in its feed. This is not a durable guaranteed retry queue.

Only the resulting article is inserted. The source name, URL and GUID are retained. There is no separate published original copy to delete. Elsewhere in the older non-city pipeline, an extraction fallback still exists; do not claim all site articles are independently rewritten or human-reviewed.
